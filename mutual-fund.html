Investment Guide

Understanding Mutual Fund Investments

What is SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan allows investors to invest a fixed amount periodically into a mutual fund scheme.

What is a Lumpsum Investment?

A lumpsum investment involves investing a larger amount into a mutual fund at one time.

What is Compounding?

Compounding occurs when investment returns generate further returns over time, helping wealth potentially grow faster over longer periods.

Are Mutual Fund Returns Guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Past performance does not guarantee future returns.
