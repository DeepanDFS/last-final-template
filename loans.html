Housing Loan

Financing to purchase, construct, or renovate a residential property — repaid over a long tenure so the monthly EMI stays manageable alongside your other goals.

About this loan

A housing loan (home loan) is a secured loan where the property being purchased or constructed is mortgaged to the lender as collateral. It can be used for a ready-to-move-in flat, an under-construction property, a self-built house, or a home extension/renovation. Interest is usually charged on a reducing balance and can be fixed, floating, or a hybrid of both.

Key features

  • Loan-to-value (LTV) of up to 75–90% of the property's agreement value, depending on the loan amount slab.
  • Tenure of up to 30 years, or until the borrower turns 65–70, whichever is earlier.
  • Balance transfer and top-up loan options once you've built repayment history.
  • Tax benefits are generally available on both principal and interest components under applicable tax provisions — please confirm current limits with a tax professional.

Eligibility & documents typically needed

  • Age: usually 21–65 years at loan maturity.
  • Stable income — salaried or self-employed/business, with at least 2–3 years of work or business continuity.
  • Reasonable credit score (lenders generally prefer 700+).
  • KYC documents, income proof (salary slips/ITRs), bank statements, and property documents (sale agreement, approved plan, title documents).

Indicative rate & fee details

Interest rate (floating)~8.30% – 9.50% p.a.
Max tenureUp to 30 years
Max LTVUp to 90%
Processing fee~0.5% – 1% of loan amount
Prepayment chargesNil on floating-rate loans for individuals*

*Rates, fees, and prepayment terms vary by lender, loan amount, credit profile, and scheme, and change over time. These figures are indicative only — we'll confirm exact terms from partner lenders once we understand your requirement.

Loan Against Property (LAP)

Unlock funds for business expansion, education, medical needs, or debt consolidation by mortgaging a residential or commercial property you already own — without selling it.

About this loan

A Loan Against Property is a secured, multi-purpose loan where an owned residential or commercial property is pledged as collateral. Because it's secured, LAP typically offers larger loan amounts and longer tenures than unsecured personal loans, at a comparatively lower interest rate — while you retain ownership and continued use of the property.

Key features

  • Funds can be used for any legitimate purpose — business, education, medical expenses, or consolidating high-cost debt.
  • Both residential and commercial properties are generally accepted as collateral.
  • Longer repayment tenure compared to unsecured loans, which keeps EMIs lower.
  • Overdraft variants are available with some lenders, where interest is charged only on the amount utilized.

Eligibility & documents typically needed

  • Clear, marketable title to the property being mortgaged.
  • Age: usually 25–65 years, salaried or self-employed.
  • Stable, demonstrable income to support repayment.
  • Property documents, income proof, bank statements, and a property valuation/legal check by the lender.

Indicative rate & fee details

Interest rate~9.00% – 12.00% p.a.
Max tenureUp to 15 years
Max LTVUp to 60% – 70%
Processing fee~0.5% – 1.5% of loan amount
Valuation/legal feeAs per lender, usually borne by applicant

*Rates, fees, and LTV depend on the property type (residential vs. commercial), location, and your credit profile. Indicative figures only — final terms are set by the lender.

Loan Against Mutual Funds (LAMF)

Get quick liquidity by pledging your existing mutual fund units as collateral — without redeeming your investments or interrupting your long-term compounding.

About this loan

A Loan Against Mutual Funds lets you borrow against equity or debt mutual fund units you already hold, by marking a lien on those units in favour of the lender through your registrar (CAMS/KFintech). It's usually offered as an overdraft facility, so you pay interest only on the amount you actually draw — not on the full sanctioned limit — while your investments stay invested and continue to grow.

Key features

  • Overdraft-style facility — interest charged only on the amount utilized, not the full limit.
  • Fast, largely digital process since units are pledged electronically; no physical property paperwork.
  • Your mutual fund investments remain invested and keep earning returns while pledged.
  • Loan limit is revised periodically based on the prevailing NAV of the pledged units.

Eligibility & documents typically needed

  • Mutual fund units held in your own name (demat or statement-of-account/SOA form) with an eligible AMC/fund.
  • Completed KYC with the fund house/registrar.
  • Basic KYC documents and a lien-marking request submitted through the registrar.
  • Some lenders exclude certain fund categories — this is confirmed at the time of application.

Indicative rate & fee details

Interest rate~9.50% – 11.50% p.a.
LTV on equity MF unitsUp to ~50%
LTV on debt MF unitsUp to ~75% – 80%
Processing fee~0.25% – 1% of sanctioned limit
Facility typeUsually renewable overdraft (12 months)

*If the NAV of pledged units falls significantly, you may face a margin call requiring top-up collateral or partial repayment. Rates and LTV vary by fund category and lender — indicative only.

Land Purchase Loan

Financing to purchase a residential plot today, with the option to add a construction loan later when you're ready to build.

About this loan

A land purchase loan finances the purchase of a plot of land intended for residential use — typically a plot within an approved layout or municipal limits. It is distinct from an agricultural land loan and from a composite home loan; many borrowers first take a land purchase loan and later add a construction component once they're ready to build.

Key features

  • Covers plots in approved residential layouts within municipal or urban development authority limits.
  • Can typically be combined with a construction loan later, subject to fresh approval.
  • Slightly higher rate and shorter tenure than a standard home loan, reflecting the absence of a completed structure.
  • Disbursement is usually linked to the registered sale deed and lender's title verification.

Eligibility & documents typically needed

  • Plot must be residential/non-agricultural with clear, marketable title and an approved layout.
  • Age: usually 21–65 years, salaried or self-employed.
  • Stable income and a reasonable credit score.
  • Sale deed/agreement, layout approval, encumbrance certificate, and standard KYC and income documents.

Indicative rate & fee details

Interest rate~8.75% – 10.25% p.a.
Max tenureUp to 15 – 20 years
Max LTVUp to 70% – 75%
Processing fee~0.5% – 1% of loan amount
Agricultural landNot eligible

*Construction must generally begin within a stipulated period (commonly 2–3 years) per some lenders' policies, and tax benefits available on home loans may not apply until construction is complete. Indicative figures only.
